Founder, GCS

GCS — Build Better Organizations.

GCS is a founder-led management and technology consulting firm. The Founder is directly involved in every client engagement — site walks, assessments, design decisions, and delivery review.

There is no layer of account management between the client and the person accountable for the work. That structure caps how many engagements the firm can run concurrently, which is an accepted constraint rather than a problem to be solved by hiring quickly.

GCS applies the same integrating role to an organization's operating model that a general contractor applies to a construction project: coordinating every system, holding every party to a schedule, resolving conflicts between domains, and delivering a finished structure that works.
