Map Intelligence Capabilities

GIS connected to enterprise intelligence.

Selectable Layers

Toggle facilities, fleet, projects, work orders, risk zones, service territories, infrastructure, and more — all on the same geographic canvas.

Connected Records

Click any map feature to see the connected operational record — condition score, open work orders, project status, alerts, and linked departments.

Condition Visualization

Facilities and assets are color-coded by condition — critical, attention, and good — creating an immediate spatial picture of organizational health.

Capital Planning Overlay

Active and planned capital projects are mapped alongside asset conditions — enabling geographically-informed investment sequencing.

Risk Mapping

Risk zones, safety incidents, compliance gaps, and operational exposures are visualized spatially — connected to the risk register in Nexus.

Service Territory Intelligence

Service areas, catchment zones, delivery routes, and jurisdictional boundaries are integrated with the operating picture.
