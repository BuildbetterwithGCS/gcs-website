Ask Your Organization a Question

Genesis lets leadership ask natural-language questions across all connected data. No analyst needed — just ask. Answers come back in plain language with the numbers behind them.

“Which facilities have the highest maintenance cost per square foot this year?”
Genesis: Top three facilities by maintenance cost per sq ft: (1) Building C — $4.82/sqft, (2) North Annex — $3.91/sqft, (3) Main Campus East Wing — $3.44/sqft. Building C is 38% above portfolio average, driven by HVAC repairs in Q2.
Step 2 of 4

Cross-Department Analysis

Genesis connects data across every department. Ask a question that spans Finance and Safety, or HR and Projects — Genesis synthesizes the answer from connected sources.

“How does overtime spending correlate with safety incidents this quarter?”
Genesis: Departments with >15% overtime showed a 2.3× higher incident rate vs. departments under 10% overtime. Q3 data shows Finance and Fleet as highest-overtime units; Safety flagged 4 of 6 Q3 incidents in those same departments.

Governed AI — Not a Black Box

Every Genesis answer shows its sources. Leaders see which data the answer is based on, so decisions are informed — not just automated.
