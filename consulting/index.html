GCS Engagement Lifecycle

Assess → Design → Implement → Improve

GCS does not deploy software before understanding the operational context. Every engagement follows a structured lifecycle.

1
Assess

Understand the current operating model, organizational structure, data quality, and priorities. Identify gaps and opportunities before touching technology.

2
Design

Establish the improved operating model. Standardize data definitions. Define accountability structures. Confirm what connected intelligence needs to show.

3
Implement

Put the agreed operating improvements into practice. Deliver the written work products in scope, support client teams, and validate each completed item before moving forward.

4
Improve

Review results against agreed measures, identify the next practical improvements, and continue advisory support only when it is included in the written engagement.

Consulting Areas

What GCS consulting addresses.

GCS consulting is not generic management advisory. It is operational and organizational consulting applied to the context of connected intelligence.

Assessment

Operational Assessment

Understand how the organization currently operates — processes, data, accountabilities, and gaps. Establish the baseline before recommending change.

Improvement

Organizational Improvement

Clarify roles, accountabilities, and decision rights. Align the organizational structure with what the connected intelligence environment needs to function.

Improvement

Process Improvement

Identify and improve the key operational processes that connected intelligence depends on. Standardize data collection. Reduce manual workarounds.

Implementation

Implementation Support

GCS can support implementation of agreed operational changes. Software configuration, migration, or system connections are included only when they are expressly written into the engagement scope and validated before use.

Performance

Performance Improvement

Use connected intelligence to identify performance gaps, track improvements, and hold departments accountable to measurable outcomes.

Technology

Technology Enablement

Assess information flow, data quality, and practical technology needs. Any proposed system connection is separately scoped and depends on technical access, authorization, and validation.

Ongoing

Ongoing Improvement

Ongoing advisory is available under a separate written scope that defines the review cadence, deliverables, responsibilities, and fee.

Advisory + Platform

Engagement Scope

Paid work begins with a written scope.

Before work starts, GCS documents the objective, deliverables, timing, client responsibilities, fee, and exclusions for the engagement.

Included Work

Defined deliverables

The written scope identifies what GCS will deliver and what information or participation the client must provide.

Boundaries

No assumed integrations

Software configuration, data migration, system connections, and ongoing support are included only when expressly listed and validated for that engagement.

Additional Work

Approved before it begins

Requests outside the agreed scope are estimated separately and require written approval before GCS performs the additional paid work.
