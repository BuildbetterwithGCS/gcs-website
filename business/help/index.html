How do I…?

Clear instructions for everyday business tasks.

Preview guidance: GCS Business is under development. These guides describe the planned workflow; they are not instructions for a live customer account. No payroll, billing, or employee data is collected on this page.

Set up my business
  1. When signup is available, create an account using your own business email.
  2. Enter your business name and choose a workspace.
  3. Review the modules you want before submitting a subscription selection.
  4. Check the estimate; access is not activated until billing is independently verified.
Add an employee
  1. Open your team administration area.
  2. Choose Invite employee and enter the intended employee identity.
  3. Assign an appropriate role and confirm the workspace.
  4. The employee will need their own verified login before accessing company information.
Clock in or out
  1. Sign in to your assigned company workspace.
  2. Open Time and choose Clock in when your shift begins.
  3. Choose Clock out when the shift ends.
  4. Review your punch history and notify your manager of mistakes.
Request time off
  1. Open Time off and select the type of leave.
  2. Choose the date and submit the request.
  3. Watch for manager approval or denial.
  4. Leave balances and payroll calculations require separately verified policies and integrations.
Approve an employee's time
  1. Sign in with an authorized manager account.
  2. Open pending time punches and check the employee and dates.
  3. Approve only completed, accurate punches.
  4. Resolve discrepancies before payroll processing in your existing compliant system.
Create and assign a work order
  1. Open Work orders and create a job with a clear description.
  2. Choose an employee who belongs to the workspace.
  3. Save the assignment and track its status.
  4. Have the assigned employee mark the job completed when finished.
Prepare an invoice
  1. Open Invoices and create a draft for a completed job.
  2. Review customer details, line items, and totals.
  3. Save the draft for authorized review.
  4. Invoice delivery, payment collection and settlement are not yet live capabilities.
Choose a subscription plan
  1. Review Core, modular and Complete options when offered.
  2. Select the tools and user allowance your team needs.
  3. Review the non-binding monthly estimate.
  4. Only a verified provider payment and server-side entitlement process can activate paid access.
