Pilot acceptance checklist

  • Release gates: exact-head CI is green, required independent reviews are approved, and the protected deployment matches the reviewed commit.
  • Isolation: two synthetic tenants prove cross-tenant reads and writes fail for every pilot module.
  • Roles: separate synthetic administrator, manager, and employee identities see only their allowed actions and records.
  • Subscription: test mode proves pending access is denied and only verified, reviewed evidence can grant an entitlement.
  • Operations: synthetic users complete time, leave, approval, work-order, invoice-status, and asset-import journeys without payroll or payment claims.
  • Assistant: every task, lead, work item, and bid update is tenant- and role-scoped; bid items retain source and freshness evidence; fabricated records are rejected.
  • Email: opt-in, frequency, deduplication, unsubscribe, audit, failure handling, and one actual test delivery are independently verified before readiness is claimed.
  • Recovery: restart, backup/restore, revocation, rollback, and failed-provider paths preserve isolation and do not duplicate actions.
  • Evidence: commits, test runs, reviewed deploy IDs, screenshots or response evidence, known gaps, and the exact next action are recorded.

Guided onboarding sequence

  1. Prerequisites: keep all real company data and contact details out of the system while any required gate above is incomplete.
  2. Synthetic company: create an invented contractor tenant with invented employees, customers, jobs, invoices, assets, and bids.
  3. Identity and access: test administrator, manager, and employee journeys; repeat denial tests from a second synthetic tenant.
  4. Core operations: complete signup in test mode, entitlement denial/activation fixtures, timekeeping, leave, approvals, work orders, invoice status, and asset onboarding.
  5. Assistant and bids: preview only permission-scoped updates with retained source/freshness evidence; do not send email or present unverified opportunities.
  6. Provider validation: verify billing and email credentials in approved test modes, signed events, delivery/failure behavior, unsubscribe handling, and rollback.
  7. Independent review: obtain required security, privacy, accessibility, billing, data, and provider review for the exact release candidate.
  8. Founder handoff: only after prerequisites pass, request the minimum explicit approval and contact/configuration details needed for a real volunteer pilot. Do not contact, invite, charge, or access the volunteer company before that point.
