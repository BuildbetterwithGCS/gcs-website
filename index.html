Step 4 of 5

Ask & Analyze

GCS Genesis lets you ask natural-language questions across all your data. You don’t need a data analyst — you just ask. The answers come back in plain language with the numbers to back them up.

“Which facilities have the highest maintenance cost per square foot this year?”
Genesis: The top three facilities by maintenance cost per sq ft are: (1) Building C — $4.82/sqft, (2) North Annex — $3.91/sqft, (3) Main Campus East Wing — $3.44/sqft. Building C is 38% above portfolio average, driven by HVAC repairs in Q2.
