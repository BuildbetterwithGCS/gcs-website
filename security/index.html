Security & Privacy

Our commitment to protecting client information, respecting privacy, and maintaining confidentiality.

Last reviewed: October 10, 2026

GCS treats security, privacy, and confidentiality as essential parts of earning and maintaining client trust.

Our Commitment

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the services we provide and the information involved. We take care to protect information, respect privacy, and reduce unnecessary exposure.

Client Information

Information is handled for agreed business purposes and in accordance with applicable obligations. Please do not send passwords, financial account details, personal identifiers, health information, or other sensitive material through public website forms.

Security Expectations

Security and privacy expectations are addressed with each client as part of the agreed engagement. GCS does not claim certifications or guarantees that have not been independently established.

Questions About Security or Privacy?

For questions about security, privacy, or engagement requirements, please contact GCS. You can also review our Privacy Policy.
