Data Security & Privacy

We value the trust our clients place in us and approach information security and privacy with care.

At GCS, protecting business information and respecting client confidentiality are important parts of our professional responsibilities. We approach data security with care, accountability, and attention to the needs of each engagement.

Our Commitment

We aim to handle information responsibly, respect privacy, and consider applicable legal and contractual obligations. The appropriate requirements for confidential or sensitive information are discussed with clients as part of defining an engagement.

Public-Sector Information

We recognize that public-sector organizations may have distinct public-records, confidentiality, and records-retention responsibilities. We work with clients to establish expectations appropriate to their obligations and the agreed scope of services.

Questions About Security or Privacy?

For questions about information handling or engagement-specific requirements, please contact GCS. You can also review our Privacy Policy for information about this website.
